OUR STORY

Bringing helpful ICT
closer to home.

MAIRE ICT SOLUTION is a local ICT service built around a simple idea: getting technology help should feel approachable. Whether you need an online task completed, a document printed or a computer skill learned, our door is open.

Based at Rakwaro Business Center, we’re proud to support our neighbours in Rakwaro and surrounding areas with practical services and a friendly, personal approach.
